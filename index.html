⛽ Cheapest Gas Ahead

Prototype — takes its route from your maps app and your fuel & mileage from the car itself.

🚗 Vehicle — read from the car

In the app, fuel level and MPG come from the vehicle itself over CarPlay / Android Auto — nothing to type. This prototype simulates that feed so you can see how the math works.

🗺️ Route — from Maps

In the app, the active route is taken from the navigation already running in Google Maps / Apple Maps. Here, enter the same start and destination.

🔑 Live prices (Google Places)

Rules: everyone sees the same price-ranked list. Club stations are tagged, never personalized. A price older than 48 hours is flagged stale. A station Google has no price for is listed last as “No live price” — never estimated.
